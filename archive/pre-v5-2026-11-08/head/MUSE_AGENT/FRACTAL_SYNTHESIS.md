## 1. The single idea

Price action is the waveform of institutional energy flow. The four densities are not metaphors; they are a
classification of *what kind of structure a thing is* (density axis) kept strictly separate from *how a trade
engages* (attack axis). Conflating the two is the root error the v5.3.3 §2.0.3 and fractal-doc §3 exist to kill:
a Fibonacci Rocket is an Air TOOL used in a Fire ATTACK. Say "Air attack" and you have already derailed.

Operational consequence: the indicator must render density (zones/shapes/levels) and attack (setup markers)
as separate visual layers that coincide at confluence, never as one blended object.

## 2. Earth (liquidity + IP) — the foundation everything anchors to

- ALL liquidity is Earth. LSZ = standard Earth; IP zone = HEAVY Earth (bedrock vs soil).
- Every other element anchors to Earth: S/D needs liquidity for fuel; Fib spiderwebs anchor to Earth points
  (wick, swing H/L, zone edges); SLs shield behind Earth; rockets trigger/enter/SL on Earth rungs.
- Thin Earth never holds entry or SL. Thin-inside-thick (thin zone inside a 4H pool) counts double — the
  container donates density. IP zones are the memory of institutional intent, the most persistent structure.
- Earth creates the conditions for its own consumption: liquidity pools invite the sweep that consumes them.

Operational consequence: E1 zone boxes are the base layer. Every E2 shape and E3 anchor must reference a
zone ID. An entry/SL/TP with no Earth anchor is fabrication (E3-NEVER rule) and must not render.

## 3. Fire (S/D) — the firepower, intrinsically linked to Earth

- Supply AND demand are both Fire. BoM = hottest (origin of the move, 100% reaction if unmitigated and virgin);
  MoM = cooling (continuation, ladder-steps ONLY, never reversal entries — guaranteed loss otherwise).
- Fire without Earth cannot move price (no fuel); Earth without Fire cannot direct it (no ignition). The only
  high-probability entry is their overlap: S/D + liquidity at the same price.
- Fire is exhaustible: after ~2 strong piercing swing moves, 1–2 full days of HTF consolidation MUST follow
  (conservation of energy). Fire is session-gated: 95% of NY swings start in the second half of NY; NY rockets
  need ≥3-pip SLs.
- Freshness is battle-counted, not time-counted: VIRGIN (0, strongest) → ACTIVE-1 (~80%) → ACTIVE-2 (~50%) →
  DEPLETED (3+, TP-only) → FORTRESS (4+ strong, phase-flips Fire→Earth) → BROKEN (pierced, terminal, role-reverses).
  A 4H MoM is one-and-done: minor reaction then likely pierce — scalp at most, never swing.

Operational consequence: the indicator must know reaction count per zone and gray-out DEPLETED/BROKEN zones as
entry anchors while keeping them as TP anchors. BoM/MoM misclassification inverts the whole trade.

## 4. Water (macro shapes) — the wave forms energy takes

- Taxonomy is mechanical, keyed to ZFP (Zone Failure Probability): SS (both bounds ZFP<30%, 2–5 sessions),
  BS (source ZFP>45% + 15-min confirm; PBS variant when ZFP>70% pierces multiples), R/RS (transitional, <1 session;
  R persisting >1 session RECLASSIFIES to SS), WL/RWL/PWL/PRWL (rejection at Hard Wall ZFP<20% / magnet), WLS
  (3+ zones in 0.30–0.50 DXY cage, 2–5 sessions, resolves to BS at very high probability).
- The default weekly storyline is SS→BS→R→WL. Chain extension is ZFP-conditioned on the TARGET zone, not on
  momentum: BS into Hard Wall (<20%) → RWL, never R/PBS — the April 20–24 error class. Every chain carries an
  audit trail (shape→trigger ZFP→shape, confidence) and a mandatory alternative chain keyed to a Decision Zone.
- Consolidation phase gates setup universe: early/mid SS = scalps both ways; late = +BS-transition; post-BS =
  reinforcements only; recovery = whiplash/recovery-surge entries. "If not near a strong 4H pressure zone, BS is
  days away — milk the 15m" (swing-scalp valley).
- Pressure reading when bias is unclear: candle size, wick direction, triangle flat-vs-sharp side, 4H tiebreak.
  Speed rule: price moves at 30–50% of expected speed; today's move is often tomorrow's (+1 day at <65% confidence).

Operational consequence: E2 is a 1H-primary layer (4H validates, 30m supplements, D1 regimes) emitting a 5-day
shape+direction+key-zone+trigger+confidence decomposition. The indicator's E2 render is a forward path, not a
history trace — and it must show the alternative branch at the Decision Zone.

## 5. Air (Fibonacci) — the trajectory mapper with no density of its own

- Fib flows anywhere like wind; anchored to two Earth points it becomes a spiderweb (a real structure floating in
  air, like a web between trees). Unanchored webs don't fail — they never existed (blown away before functioning).
- Self-reference (wormholes): 0.382↔1.382, 0.618↔1.618 (strongest, highest-frequency reaction), 0.786↔1.786.
  Same math at every scale: 4H swing path, 1H entry/SL/TP, 15m scalp, 30m FLR trigger — one structure, all altitudes.
- Key bands: 0.618–0.786 primary entry (strong pressure); 1.382–1.786 cleanest reinforcement (90% with Fib lines);
  2.382–2.786 trip-wire trigger. FLR = 30m Fib trigger + liquidity entry/SL (Earth-heavy); FR = 15m Fib for LSS.
- Air↔Fire duality: Air PIERCES Fire (4H BoM S/D), then either fuels the inferno or blows out the candle —
  the whiplash mechanism. Air accelerates the system (efficient mapping, faster execution, hardwired precision).

Operational consequence: Fib levels render only from pinned Earth anchors (two-point: entry-zone S/R + nearest
swing H/L). Any Fib render without visible anchors is wind, not web — do not draw it.

## 6. The cycle and the Avatar State

Earth→Fire→Water→Air→Earth: pools form → S/D ignites at an Earth anchor → energy materializes as a shape →
spiderweb maps the full trajectory → acted-on Fib levels become new liquidity (self-fulfilling → new Earth).
Supply-flows-into-demand and BoM-to-BoM are the same cycle seen from the Fire side; ladders (1D Earth→Earth)
evolve into spiderwebs (multi-dimensional Air→Earth→Water→Fire).

E + FLR = Avatar State: entry at Earth+Fire overlap, confirmed by Water (shape), trajectory + reinforcements by
Air+Earth. Four independent ~80% confirmations intersect → joint-failure ≈0.16%. Up to 7 rockets, max 3 per pair;
SL cascade makes reinforcements risk-free once the parent is at breakeven (trip-wire stop-limit mechanics:
trigger zone ≠ entry zone; unfilled limit = zero added risk).

Operational consequence: the indicator's E3 layer must visually decompose each setup into its four element legs
so a missing leg is SEEN (no Avatar claim without all four).
