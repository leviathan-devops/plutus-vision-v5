
## **Zone Element Mechanics** 

Each timeframe’s liquidity is its own element and more likely to react to liquidity that matches its element. 

4H liquidity --> 4H liquidity 1H liquidity --> 1H liquidity. 30m --> 30m liquidity 

LTF liquidity is more likely to be “absorbed” by HTF liquidity - but usually never more than 2 dimensions of liquidity up. 

Examples: 

- Surge begins on 15m liquidity ends on 30m liquidity 

- Surge begins on 15m liquidity ends on 1H liquidity 

- Whiplash begins on 1H liquidity ends on 4H liquidity 

- Whiplash begins on 15m liquidity ends on 1H liquidity 

## **1H & 4H Liquidity react to one another as similar elements.** 

**1H/4H Liquidity can be used interchangeable with 4H liquidity being the dominant pressure zone in unmitigated BoM areas**
