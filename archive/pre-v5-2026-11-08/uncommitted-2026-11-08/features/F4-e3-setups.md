# F4 · E3 — the position boxes and the table
## Surface
Per emitted setup: a red SL box, a green TP box, the entry at their shared edge, and a table row.
## Contract
entry = the shared edge; SL = opposite edge ± 2–5 pips; TP = the nearest opposing zone on the path; RRR ≥ 2.5; ≤8 per week, ≤3 per day.
## Failure
Boxes that do not share an edge; a 2.4 RRR emitted; a 9th setup; two setups on one (zone, direction, day).
## Proof
G2: entry equals both boxes' shared boundary within one tick · G1: the red/green boxes are present and non-overlapping in the table area · G4: `entry_labels` match the card.
