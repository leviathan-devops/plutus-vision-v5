### R2 — BoM/MoM definitions and Engine 3 markings [L1147-L1152]

> `For every IP zone, classify as **BoM (Beginning of Move)** or **MoM (Middle of Move)**:` [L1147]

> `- **BoM**: The zone that ORIGINATED the directional move — the FIRST zone at the start of a trend leg. Identified by: zone is at the beginning of a clear impulse move, price left the zone with momentum, the zone is the "source" of the move.` [L1148]

> `- **MoM**: A zone that formed MID-MOVE as a continuation — not the origin, but a reaction point along the path. Identified by: zone formed during an ongoing move, not at the start, price paused here then continued.` [L1149]

> `- **BoM zones**: Mark as `ENTRY-PRIMARY` in Engine 3. 100% guaranteed reaction if unmitigated.` [L1150]

> `- **MoM zones**: Mark as `TP-TARGET` for opposing direction in Engine 3. Entry ONLY with exceptions (see Engine 3 Phase 1).` [L1151]

> `- **4H MoM specific**: Mark as `ONE-AND-DONE` — first retest = minor reaction, then likely strong pierce. Not valid for swing entries.` [L1152]

### R3 — Zone Reaction Counter heading and buckets [L1154-L1159]

> `### Zone Reaction Counter (NEW — CRITICAL)` [L1154]

> `For each IP zone, track the number of strong reactions observed:` [L1156]

> `- **0 reactions (Virgin)**: Zone has never been tested. Strongest if BoM (100% guaranteed reaction). DEATH ZONE if MoM (unpredictable reaction — could be minor then pierce).` [L1157]

> `- **1-2 reactions (Active)**: Zone still has juice. Valid for entries if BoM. Valid for entries if MoM with exceptions. Still likely to cause a reaction.` [L1158]

> `- **3+ reactions (Depleted)**: Zone is likely to fail on next test. Use for TP targeting only — NOT for entries. If zone is at 3+ reactions and is between current PA and a deeper BoM zone, price will likely pierce through it to reach the BoM zone.` [L1159]
